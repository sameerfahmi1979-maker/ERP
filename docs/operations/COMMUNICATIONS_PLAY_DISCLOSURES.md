# ALGT ERP System communications policy and Google Play release evidence

Status: draft for owner review. The policy RPC deliberately reports no current version until the approved terms are entered through a controlled release change. The static `/communications-disclosure.html` is the in-product draft. The Play listing requires a separately approved, publicly accessible privacy policy and privacy contact before submission.

## Terms draft for approval

Use ALGT ERP System company communications for authorized work. Harassment, threats, hate or discrimination, sexual or exploitative material, impersonation, spam, and unauthorized disclosure of sensitive information are prohibited. Use the relevant message, profile or Walkie-Talkie menu to report a concern. A personal block prevents new direct communication across chat and private Walkie-Talkie and suppresses targeted alerts between the two people in shared threads. Shared and Required channels and channel-wide SOS continue according to channel and availability rules. The company may restrict communications or hide policy-violating content from ordinary views while retaining the evidence for authorized review.

Named company reviewers can review private and group records within approved company and branch scopes covering every participant. Global System administrators can review across companies and branches. Existing permission rules govern ordinary-chat exports and attachment access. Walkie-Talkie participants and authorized reviewers use in-app playback. Complete transmissions and SOS notices, including an available permitted one-time location, form part of the authorized history. The microphone operates for deliberate voice-note recording or holding Talk while authorized. An active Walkie-Talkie session can receive audio in the background and can be stopped through the app's active controls.

Use the applicable approved privacy policy and supported contact process for questions about company retention and privacy. This draft needs the owner's approval before assigning a current terms version. A published version becomes immutable. Changes require a new version and a new affirmative acceptance before further contributions.
