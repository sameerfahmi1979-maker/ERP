ALGT ERP System communication privacy

Draft for company review. The approved privacy policy and privacy contact must be published before the Google Play release.

Company messages and Walkie-Talkie

ALGT ERP System uses your ERP account and work identity for company communications. The communications service handles messages, attachments, voice notes, saved Walkie-Talkie transmissions, related delivery information and abuse reports. Available directories are intended for company work contacts. Existing ERP permissions continue to control HR records and documents.

Participants can see the conversations they are permitted to access. Named company reviewers can review private and group history when their approved company and branch assignments cover every participant. Global System administrators can review company history across companies and branches. Authorized ordinary-chat history and files may be exported or downloaded. Authorized Walkie-Talkie replay is available inside the app.

Microphone and location

A voice note uses the microphone while you record. During an active Walkie-Talkie session, holding Talk uses the microphone after speaking permission is granted. Complete transmissions are sent and saved for replay. An active session can receive audio when the app is in the background. The ongoing Walkie-Talkie control includes Stop.

When you send SOS, the app can include one available approximate device location if you allow it. Eligible members of your selected channel can view the alert. The alert and included location remain in authorized history after cancellation. The app can send SOS without a location if a permitted current location is unavailable.

Reporting, blocking and retained history

Use the menu on a message, profile or Walkie-Talkie item to report a concern. Assigned moderators receive cases. Access to the supporting conversation follows the separate company history rules. A personal block stops new direct communication between the two accounts, including private Walkie-Talkie and targeted alerts in shared conversations. Shared channels and channel-wide SOS follow their normal access and notification rules.

Deleting a chat message under the applicable controls removes it from ordinary participant viewing and retains it for authorized review. Removing a saved copy affects the local device. Blocking and SOS cancellation preserve the applicable company history. The final privacy policy will explain retention and how to contact the company about your data.
